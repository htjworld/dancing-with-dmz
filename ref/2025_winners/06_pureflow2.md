## Challenge (excerpt)

Space habitats are “homes in space” that keep crew members healthy and able to execute their mission. Whether located on a planetary surface or in space, habitats must support critical functions such as waste management, thermal control, life support, communications, power, stowage, food storage and preparation, medical care, sleep, and exercise. Space habitat concepts can involve a diverse array of materials, geometries, and layouts. Your challenge is to create a visual tool that enables users to define a space habitat’s shape/volume and explore possible layout options. (Exploration Systems Development Mission Directorate)

## Project summary

We developed PureFlow, an interactive systems engineering platform that allows users to design, model in 3D, and validate space habitats. It directly addresses the challenge by enabling the creation of layouts and volumes, but it goes further: the system calculates critical crew requirements in real-time (mass, power, life support) based on crew size and mission duration, providing instant feedback on the design's viability.

Its importance lies in being more than a layout tool; it is a survival simulator. With its unique integration of NASA's DONKI API, PureFlow tests the design against real space weather threats, such as solar storms. It doesn't just answer if a habitat can be built, but if its crew can survive in it, making resilient design a fundamental priority.

## Project details

PureFlow: Would Your Habitat Survive a Solar Storm... Today?
We are standing on the brink of interplanetary civilization. But between our dreams of Martian colonies and lunar bases lies a brutal reality: space is unforgiving. It’s not enough to build a shelter; we must engineer a self sustaining ecosystem. PureFlow is more than a design tool. It's the crucible where your deep space habitat is tested against the harsh, unpredictable truths of the cosmos.

What is PureFlow?
PureFlow is your command center for mission architecture. This interactive platform empowers you to design, simulate, and validate a complete space habitat for any destination, such as the Moon, Mars, or deep space. Step into the role of Mission Architect. From the initial briefing to the final 3D model, PureFlow is your dedicated systems engineer, instantly analyzing the ripple effects of every choice you make.

Features That Define the Future:
From Blueprint to Reality in 3D
Forget static blueprints. With PureFlow, you sculpt your habitat in a dynamic 3D environment. Place crew quarters, connect life support, and position science labs. With every click, our physics engine provides immediate feedback, calculating critical shifts in mass, power draw, and internal volume. You don't just design a habitat; you watch it come alive.

The Logistics of Life
A crew of four on Mars needs tons of supplies. Or do they? PureFlow runs a deep survival simulation, modeling the entire life support loop. Integrate advanced ECLSS or a greenhouse, and see the numbers change in real time. Witness exactly how recycling and in situ resources can slash your launch mass, turning an impossible mission into a feasible one.

Armor Against the Elements
A Martian dust storm can shred exposed materials. Lunar radiation is relentless. Your habitat's skin is its first and last line of defense. Choose your armor, from advanced titanium composites to 3D printed native regolith. PureFlow’s analysis engine immediately pits your choice against the specific environmental hazards of your destination, revealing vulnerabilities before they become fatal.

The Decisive Difference: A Live Link to the Sun
This is what truly sets PureFlow apart. We are plugged directly into NASA's DONKI, the Space Weather Database. This isn't a historical model. It's live. When a solar flare erupts from the sun right now, an alert flashes across your screen. Your habitat is hit with the full force of the storm in your simulation. Is the shielding enough? Is the storm shelter positioned correctly? PureFlow doesn't just prepare you for a theoretical threat. It tests your creation against the cosmos as it is, today.

Our Mission: Engineering for Resilience
The next giant leap depends on tools that are as tough, smart, and adaptable as our astronauts. PureFlow was born from this conviction. We fuse immersive 3D design with rigorous systems engineering and live, unpredictable data from space itself. We exist to answer the only question that matters: Is your crew's new home ready?
