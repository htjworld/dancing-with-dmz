## Challenge (excerpt)

Although the Sun is 93 million miles away from our planet, solar activity can significantly impact our daily lives. “Space weather”—the variations that occur in the space environment between the Sun and Earth—can impact technologies in space and on Earth. Your challenge is to write and illustrate a digital children’s story that explains what space weather is and the varying impacts it has on different people such as farmers, pilots, astronauts, power grid operators, and the general public. You can tell the story from the perspective of a person impacted by space weather or from the point of view of a solar flare or coronal mass ejection (CME) as it approaches Earth. (Heliophysics Division)

## About the team

We are HerCode Space, a creative and tech-driven collective of women passionate about turning data into meaningful stories.

## Project summary

With A Solar Tale, we wanted the Sun to tell its own story — to children and to anyone fascinated by the universe, its mythology, history, and science.
Our project addresses the “Stellar Stories” challenge by combining NASA data and heliophysics concepts with powerful storytelling, vibrant illustrations, and accessible language for young learners. 
This story makes complex science human, emotional, and memorable — helping kids understand how space weather affects daily life and why it matters. It’s a bridge between science and imagination, designed for use in classrooms, libraries, and outreach programs.

## Project details

🌞 Details 
A Solar Tale is a digital children's story told from the perspective of the Sun — a cosmic narrator who speaks across cultures, centuries, and technologies. Through five poetic acts, the story traces humanity’s relationship with space weather, highlighting how solar activity affects life on Earth. From ancient myths to Galileo's telescope, and from space flares to a young girl’s curiosity, the tale blends science and storytelling.
